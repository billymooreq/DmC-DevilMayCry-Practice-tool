DmC Practice Tool v{VERSION} (beta) - for DmC: Devil May Cry, Steam version v1.3 and v1.0

How to install:
1. In Steam: right-click DmC Devil May Cry > Manage > Browse local files.
2. Open the folder Binaries\Win32.
3. Put dinput8.dll from this zip there (next to DMC-DevilMayCry.exe).
4. Start the game and press Insert to open the menu.
5. To remove the tool, delete dinput8.dll from that folder.

Help, features and questions: https://github.com/billymooreq/DmC-DevilMayCry-Practice-tool

Unofficial fan project, not connected to Capcom or Ninja Theory. Use at your own risk and back up your saves.
